# CTABand

A rounded Forest card that asks once more: two-tone `t-h2`, a lead and two buttons, with a large leaf shape bleeding off the corner.

## Use
- Markup in `components/_partials/cta.html`, inside a Light section's container. One per page, after the gallery.
- Actions: large primary **Offerte aanvragen** and the phone as an outline button.

## Svelte
`CTABand.svelte`: `title`, `accentWord`, `lead`, `primary: {label, href}`, `phone`.

## Avoid
- Placing it directly next to another Forest section.
- Cards or photos inside the band.
